Project Overview

Max-Cut divides graph nodes into two groups to maximize the total weight of edges connecting different groups. QAOA combines a parameterized quantum circuit with classical optimization to search for high-quality solutions.

01 · Graph Define nodes, edges, and weights.
02 · Hamiltonian Encode the Max-Cut objective.
03 · QAOA Optimize gamma and beta parameters.
04 · Benchmark Compare cut quality and circuit resources.
